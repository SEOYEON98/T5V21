## Attribution

When sharing or adapting these datasets or result artifacts, users should:
1. Credit **National Institute of Environmental Research (NIER), Republic of Korea**.
2. Cite the associated T5V21 research paper when its full citation becomes available.
3. Link to the dataset release and to https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
4. Indicate whether changes were made.

## Publication status and rights

The uploaded GitHub Release assets are currently saved as a **draft**.
They are not available to the public until the release is published.

Before publication, the releasing researcher must confirm that NIER has the
right to grant CC BY 4.0 reuse rights for all included material and that the
release assets contain no restricted or third-party material. If some files
have different rights or restrictions, list those exceptions explicitly
rather than applying this notice to them.
